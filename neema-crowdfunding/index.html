Our story

Every week, someone walks in with a song in their heart and nothing in their pocket.

A young worshipper from a church choir. A teenager who writes lyrics in an exercise book. A single mother whose voice lights up her village. They have the gift. What they don't have is the money for a recording session, a music video, or someone to guide them.

Neema Pro Studios exists to discover, nurture and promote talent with excellence, integrity and faith. It is also the home of the Neema Pro Studios Choir. But we can't say yes to everyone who needs us. With your help, we can say yes to many more.

"Kwa Neema tu": only by grace. This is grace in action.
